Voice-over · 0:39–1:09 · estimated

So we built Intatto, a lending market that lives natively on X Layer. You just put in your tokenized stocks and borrow USDG against them, any day of the week.

The key thing is that Intatto knows when the actual stock market is open. Your borrowing limit tracks the market session: it's higher while the stock is actually trading, and it tightens once the market closes.

And the liquidation threshold never moves, so a market close never pushes an existing loan toward liquidation.

Voice-over · 3:02–3:22 · estimated

The model is pretty simple: borrowers pay interest, eighty percent of it goes to lenders, and twenty percent goes into the gap reserve. We make money by running the keeper, which earns half of the five percent liquidation penalty, and the credit API is built for pay-per-call through x402, so agents can pay per query once we switch it on.

Every stock is its own isolated market, so adding the next stock is just a deployment away. We already run NVDAx and SPYx on our fork, and NVDAx on mainnet.

Production notes

Five acts, one spoken explanation. The week is the demo’s camera path; each transition serves the next idea.

Week timeline

Dive into the market week.

Friday → Saturday → January 2025 / Monday’s open. Pull out of each scene to the week, move time, then dive into the next one. White means open; black means closed. Do not show sandbox time controls or UI toggles.

The session dial anchors time; it is no longer a repeated wipe at every cut. Preserve the hero dial and its fixed 65% threshold.

Transitions

A distinct transition for each idea.

Token line → pricing gap → Intatto name → product flow → X Layer centre. The fork block becomes the week; the replay trace becomes the keeper comparison; reserve loss becomes interest allocation; a market tile clears into chat.

Keep the movement continuous and causal. No repeated generic whooshes; one purposeful sound per action, and quiet holds.

Composition

Fewer containers, larger evidence.

Use open space and one dominant idea per shot. Avoid a grid of boxed explanations. Containers belong to actual UI, the two isolated markets and the agent conversation. Real market visuals should carry the risk section where possible.

System UI type; black, white and neutral greys. Red for loss/refusal and green for pass. Keep source labels and fork/replay context legible.

X Layer centre

Mainnet on one side. The fork on the other.

Beat 4 centres the approved X Layer logo, with Intatto’s actual mainnet addresses and live loan to the left, and the fork at block 71,563,431 to the right. Zoom from the logo into each side.

The wireframe marks logo placement, not a replacement brand asset. This is the one explanation of live mainnet versus the fork before the demo.
