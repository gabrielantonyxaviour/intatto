## The film
A ~3:30 product film for OKX Dev Day judges (Intatto: borrowing against tokenized stocks on X Layer, with limits
that know when the stock market is closed). Narrated by Gabriel, so the music must be instrumental only, with no
vocals under the voice-over.
- **0:00–~0:40, cold open:** cinematic and tense. Friday's closing bell, a weekend in darkness, the Monday crash.
- **Middle (~2:20):** clean, confident, modern. Stripe or Linear launch-film energy for the product and the proof.
- **Close (~0:25):** uplifting and resolved.
- A pulse or clock feel is a plus (the motif is a session clock). It should cut well at about **90–110 BPM**.
