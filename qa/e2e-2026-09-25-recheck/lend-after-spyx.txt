Borrowers pay 2.00% a year at zero utilisation, rising by 8.00% up to the 80% kink, then by a further 100.00% between the kink and full utilisation, so idle USDG comes back quickly. Lenders receive the borrow rate × utilisation, less the 20% that funds the gap reserve.

Risk
Loss risk
Lenders can lose money in a gap larger than the reserve. There is no insurance fund beyond the gap reserve. If NVDAx or SPYx opens far below where it closed and a liquidation cannot recover the debt, the gap reserve pays first; any remainder lowers the value of every lender's shares, in proportion to what they hold. SPYx is sandbox-only.
Who pays for a loss, in order
1. Recovered collateral

770.11 USDG

An unhealthy position's collateral is sold into the pool in bounded slices. The proceeds repay its debt and the 5% penalty first. Right now 770.11 USDG of collateral backs 10.4 USDG of debt (1.4% loan-to-value across all borrowers).

2. Gap reserve

100.50 USDG

If the collateral is gone and debt is left, the gap reserve pays the rest straight into the vault, up to its balance.

3. Lenders, pro rata

50,001.49 USDG

Whatever the reserve cannot pay is written off. Every share loses the same fraction. Each 1,000 USDG written off lowers the share price by 1.999% at today's deposits.
