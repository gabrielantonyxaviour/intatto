Trust and bounds
Trust and bounds

What you are trusting when you use Intatto, and where the contracts stop that trust.

The keeper is a trusted relayer

The NVDAx price is the issuer's indicative quote, relayed by Intatto's keeper (
0xaBa1…f808
(opens OKLink)
). The quote carries no source timestamp, so the console shows when the keeper fetched it, never how fresh the stock price is.

It can
post the market session, the price, the credit cap target and upcoming corporate actions; a wrong session post can open borrowing up to the OPEN limit, never beyond it
run liquidation slices (anyone can; the keeper usually does)
stop posting, which turns borrowing off once its posts go stale
The contracts stop it from
moving anyone's collateral, deposits or the reserve
posting a price outside the TWAP band, beyond the max move, or while USDG is off peg
lending past the OPEN session's limit, or raising the cap faster than its ramp
selling collateral below the liquidation floor

The operator (
0xaBa1…f808
(opens OKLink)
) can change these bounds and the keeper address; every change emits an event.

Bounds read onchain

Each mechanism and the number the contract enforces now.

Price freshness
30 min
A price older than this is stale: new borrowing and liquidation stop until the keeper posts again.
Fetch age at post
5 min
The keeper must post within this long of fetching the quote.
TWAP band
±3% open · ±8% otherwise
A post outside the band around the pool's 30-minute TWAP is rejected.
Max move per post
15%
A bigger jump from the last accepted price is rejected; the keeper has to step towards it.
USDG/USD peg
within 1%, fresh within 26 h
Checked against Chainlink USDG/USD on every post.
Session liveness
30 min
Without a session post for this long the market reads UNKNOWN and lends nothing new.
New-borrow LTV
50% / 40% / 30%→20%
OPEN / EXTENDED / CLOSED start → floor. HALTED, CORPORATE_ACTION and UNKNOWN allow 0%.
Liquidation LTV
65%
Fixed in the market contract, the same in every session.
Liquidation penalty
5%
Of the debt repaid; half to whoever ran the slice, half to the gap reserve.
Liquidation floor
15% open · 3% closed
A slice never sells below the relayed price minus this. While CLOSED a waiting slice widens to 8% after 6 h.
Cap ramp
25% or 100 USDG per hour
How fast a higher cap target takes effect. Lower targets apply at once.
Corporate action pause
30 min
Borrowing and liquidation pause this long before a multiplier change activates.
Reserve factor
20%
Share of borrower interest kept for the protocol instead of lenders.
A liquidation, worked through

Say a loan has 1,000 USDG of NVDAx collateral and 700 USDG of debt: 70% LTV, past the 65% line. The liquidator seizes enough wrapper shares to cover the debt plus the 5% penalty and sells them into the pool, never below the floor. Proceeds repay the 700 USDG; the penalty (35 USDG) goes half to the caller and half to the gap reserve; anything left returns to the borrower. If the collateral runs out first, the gap reserve pays the rest and only then do lenders share a deficit.
