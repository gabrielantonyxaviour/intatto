How the price is built

Layered and checked onchain on every post.

The issuer's indicative NVDAx quote, fetched by Intatto's keeper. It has no source timestamp, so the relay records when the keeper fetched it and refuses fetches older than 5 min.
Converted to a wrapper-share price with the wrapper's own share-to-token rate (the issuer multiplier is inside it, never applied twice).
Checked against the wrapper/USDG pool's 30 min TWAP: within ±3% while the market is OPEN, ±8% otherwise.
Capped at 15% of movement from the last accepted post, and refused while Chainlink USDG/USD is stale or more than 1% off $1.
